# Precious Elochukwu Awuzu

**Healthcare operations, data science and service improvement** · [Explore my portfolio](https://precious-awuzu.github.io/)

In NHS occupational health, people called back after receiving a referral letter because “shortly” gave them little idea when to expect an appointment. I rewrote the wording to explain the expected 12-week wait. When appointment changes could reach either administrators or doctors’ PAs, I built a shared Microsoft Lists tracker so both teams could see which requests still needed review.

That experience shapes the questions I ask of data. I want to know who is missing from a satisfaction score, what a referral count tells a clinic manager, and whether a more complicated model earns its place. I bring an MSc in Data Science (Merit) from the University of Gloucestershire, experience resolving customer issues and coaching colleagues at Three UK, administrative team leadership in Nigeria, and frontline experience in residential care.
