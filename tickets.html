Customer support tickets in R

I reviewed 8,469 tickets across four channels. Email and phone volumes differed by just 11 tickets.

Channel demand

Email accounted for 2,143 tickets, phone for 2,132, social media for 2,121 and chat for 2,073. Ticket volumes were similar across channels. Staffing decisions would also need handling time and arrival patterns.

Interpreting satisfaction scores

Only 2,769 tickets had a satisfaction rating. The other 5,700 had no score. A summary of recorded scores describes respondents and may differ from the experience of customers who gave no rating.
