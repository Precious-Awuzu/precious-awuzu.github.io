Improvements in NHS occupational health

Why were people calling back?

Our referral letters said people would hear from us “shortly”. Callers wanted to know whether they had been forgotten. I changed the wording to give the expected 12-week wait. People had a timeframe they could plan around, and repeat enquiries fell.

Which appointment requests still needed review?

Urgent requests and appointment changes could reach either the admin team or the doctors’ PAs. I built a shared Microsoft Lists tracker so either team could log a request and see whether it was new, under review or complete.

What happens when two records disagree?

When I found a discrepancy in pathology information, I contacted the client to check the details and corrected the occupational health record. That gave colleagues accurate information for the next review.

NHS referral demand by week, priority, specialty and commissioner

I analysed 1,674,089 referrals recorded from 1 to 28 September 2025 across 62 specialties and 114 commissioner entries. Routine referrals made up 66.5% of demand overall and 85.2% in orthopaedics. The ten highest-volume commissioners accounted for 29.35% of all referrals.
